# 1. Executive Summary

The recruiter subscription model should be competitive against
mainstream hiring platforms while still protecting Skill Ecosystem's
differentiated value. Recruiters are used to low-friction job posting,
resume/contact access, sponsored visibility and sales-led enterprise
hiring. Therefore, a recruiter model that starts only at a high annual
fee will create unnecessary entry friction.

The recommended approach is to allow recruiter onboarding through a Free
plan, convert small recruiters through a low-cost Lite plan, and
monetize serious hiring through contact unlocks, job boosts, assessment
insights, AI shortlists, campus hiring campaigns and enterprise
contracts.

Skill Ecosystem should not position itself as another generic resume
database. Its strongest market positioning is: assessed, verified,
early-career and institution-linked talent with skill-readiness data and
portfolio proof.

# 2. Competitive Context and Pricing Implication

The model below is based on the competitive pattern that recruiters
already understand:

- Free or low-friction job posting to test applicant flow.

- Paid resume/contact access or candidate outreach credits for sourcing.

- Sponsored jobs, employer branding and hiring campaigns for higher
  visibility.

- Enterprise pricing for large teams, volume hiring, integrations and
  managed services.

| **Competitor buying behaviour**                                                   | **Implication for Skill Ecosystem**                                                                           |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Free/sponsored posting models exist in the market                                 | Keep a Free recruiter plan and avoid forcing annual payment before recruiters see applicant quality.          |
| Resume/contact database access is usually monetized separately                    | Use contact unlock credits and invite credits instead of unlimited candidate access.                          |
| Employer branding and visibility are commonly separate paid products              | Sell job boosts, featured jobs, featured employer branding and hiring campaigns as add-ons.                   |
| Enterprise hiring depends on seats, hiring volume, integrations and service level | Keep Enterprise as “From INR 75,000/year” with custom configuration.                                          |
| Large generic job boards compete on database volume                               | Skill Ecosystem should compete on verified readiness, assessments, portfolios, and institution-linked talent. |

# 6. Credit-Based Commercial Model

The subscription should define the recruiter relationship, while credits
should control high-value consumption. This protects learner data,
avoids unlimited database harvesting, and creates a scalable revenue
path for active recruiters.

Credits should be visible inside the recruiter dashboard with used,
available, expired and expiring-soon balances.

| **Credit Type**            | **Used For**                                               | **Recommended Rule**                                                    |
|----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Contact Unlock Credits     | Unlock phone/email or direct contact access.               | 1 credit per candidate contact unlock. No contact access in Free plan.  |
| Invite Credits             | Invite learners to apply for a role.                       | 1 credit per invite. Included invite limits vary by plan.               |
| Assessment Insight Credits | View deeper candidate assessment/readiness evidence.       | 1-2 credits based on depth; full report available in Pro or Enterprise. |
| Job Boost Credits          | Improve job visibility for a defined period.               | 1 boost credit per job boost event; included only from Starter upward.  |
| AI Shortlist Credits       | Generate role-specific candidate shortlists.               | Add-on for lower plans; included/limited in Growth; included in Pro.    |
| Campus Access Credits      | Access institution-linked hiring pools or drive campaigns. | Add-on for Growth/Pro; included/custom in Enterprise.                   |

# 7. Credit Expiry, Rollover and Over-Limit Rules

| **Rule Area**             | **Recommended Behaviour**                                                                                                            |
|---------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Included annual credits   | Valid during the active subscription year. Unused included credits expire at renewal unless explicitly carried over by policy.       |
| Monthly allocated credits | Roll over for 3 months if the subscription remains active.                                                                           |
| Purchased add-on credits  | Valid for 6 months from purchase date.                                                                                               |
| Expired subscription      | Credits are frozen during grace period and expire if the subscription is not renewed.                                                |
| Downgrade                 | Purchased credits remain usable until expiry; included plan limits immediately follow the lower plan after downgrade effective date. |
| Over-limit usage          | Block action and prompt upgrade/top-up. Do not allow silent overages unless the account has approved credit terms.                   |
| Refund treatment          | Consumed credits are non-refundable. Unused add-on credits may be handled as per refund policy.                                      |

# 11. Subscription Lifecycle Rules

| **Lifecycle Area**  | **Recommended Behaviour**                                                                                                                       |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Trial / Free plan   | No expiry for Free, but high-value actions are restricted. Company verification required for job publishing.                                    |
| Upgrade             | Immediate activation after successful payment. New posts/seats/credits become available immediately.                                            |
| Proration           | For annual plan upgrades, charge differential based on remaining term where billing system supports it; otherwise activate a fresh annual term. |
| Downgrade           | Apply downgrade at next renewal by default. If immediate downgrade is requested, retain data but restrict premium actions.                      |
| Subscription expiry | Provide a 7-day grace period for paid plans. During grace, existing jobs remain visible but new premium actions are blocked.                    |
| Renewal             | Manual renewal by default for Indian B2B; auto-renewal optional only where payment mandate and consent are configured.                          |
| Cancellation        | Stop future renewal. Access remains until paid term end unless policy grants immediate cancellation.                                            |
| Refund              | No refund after substantial usage/credit consumption. Time-bound refund can be offered only before material use, as per Finance policy.         |
| Failed payment      | Notify immediately, retry where supported, then move account to grace/restricted state.                                                         |

# 15. Final Recommendation

Adopt the competitive recruiter model with Free, Lite, Starter, Growth,
Pro and Enterprise tiers. Show only Free, Lite, Growth, Pro and
Enterprise on the main public pricing screen to keep the UI simple. Keep
Starter as an intermediate plan in the full comparison table, upgrade
flow or sales conversation.

Use INR 2,500/year as the lowest public paid recruiter subscription.
This supports market entry while preserving monetization through credits
and add-ons.

Make Growth the recommended paid plan for regular hiring. Use the badge
“Most Popular for Active Hiring”.

Position Skill Ecosystem as a verified skill-based hiring platform:
assessed talent, readiness scores, portfolio evidence, and
institution-linked talent pools. This differentiates it from generic job
boards that primarily sell posting and resume access.
